Thanks for stopping by.

Kivi Studio is Ela Siwy's playground. It's an umbrella term (and a [UK Limited company](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13469571)) for all my experiments, aspiring to become a proper Studio (whatever that means). Here I aim to demonstrate that we can design differently – slowly, using scientific methodology, valuing human joy over productivity.

Currently, I am working on a family of products - [[Marginalia]]. Marginalia is a family of projects that make it easier to catalogue, share, and learn from physical books. It's built on the idea that reading books together creates shared knowledge and sparks better conversations.

If you have a library that needs cataloguing, [[The Home Librarian]] might be for you.

Like what you see? Have any ideas for collaborations?
